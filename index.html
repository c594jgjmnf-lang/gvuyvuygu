THE ART OF CARE / 02

To love
is to care.

Caring for a horse is a serious commitment. Comfort, movement, proper nutrition and health monitoring are essential to animal welfare.

“The first step to understanding a horse is learning to listen.”
01 Balanced Nutrition+

A horse’s diet should be built around quality forage, especially hay or pasture grass. Clean water must always be available. Choose concentrates, salt and supplements with professional guidance for each horse’s needs.

02 Movement & Companionship+

Horses are social animals that need movement and contact with other horses. Daily turnout and safe living conditions support both physical and mental wellbeing.

03 Hooves & Grooming+

Inspect and clean hooves regularly, and check the skin and coat. A qualified farrier should set the trimming and shoeing schedule based on the individual horse and its workload.

04 Health & Safety+

Discuss routine examinations, vaccinations, parasite control and dental care with your veterinarian. Signs of colic, severe lameness or unusual behaviour warrant prompt veterinary attention.
